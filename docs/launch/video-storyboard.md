# Glyph launch video storyboard

Film real phone-to-panel responses. Use the build intended for launch and note its version, phone, WLED version and panel size in the edit log. The plans below use a single 16×16 panel, avoiding unverified multi-panel claims. Android is the filmed platform; no iOS footage or availability claim.

For the phone-off shot, allow Android to finish shutting down and record a longer continuous take, then use its final five seconds. A dark screen alone would not prove the stream stopped. Include “iOS untested” in the post description.

### Vertical 9:16 variant

Deliver 1080×1920. Film with the panel in the upper middle and phone below, both near the optical plane. Keep captions in the middle safe area, away from the bottom 20% and right-side platform controls. Use a full-width screen-recording insert for size/review only; return to the real two-shot for every response. Keep the repository URL on two lines if necessary.

### Landscape 16:9 variant

Deliver 1920×1080. Film phone left and panel right with a neutral gap for captions. Use the same 40-second timing, with close-up inserts framed separately in landscape. Place the final URL across the lower middle with room for player controls. Capture both orientations at the camera; a crop of the landscape two-shot will usually lose either the phone or panel in vertical.

## Capture checklist

- [ ] Record build/version details; confirm every shown feature exists in the downloadable launch build. Record single-panel physical footage; label any development-build footage explicitly.
- [ ] Snapshot `/json/state`, `/presets.json` and `/cfg.json` before changing the filming device. Use test-owned art/files; restore configuration/playback afterwards and remove only test-created Saved looks/files. Have setup undo ready.
- [ ] Lower panel brightness until colours retain detail. Start around 10–25%, then adjust for the camera and room; full brightness commonly clips LED colour.
- [ ] Use a dim room with soft fill on the hand and phone, plus a diffuser on the panel if available. Avoid bright reflections and an entirely black phone silhouette.
- [ ] Lock manual exposure, focus and white balance. Test 30 fps with 1/60 s, then 1/30 s if LED banding persists; check footage frame by frame. Choose a shutter that works with this panel/camera rather than assuming one setting removes all PWM flicker.
- [ ] Avoid moiré by changing distance or angle slightly, keeping the camera square enough to compare geometry, and avoiding aggressive sharpening. Use a tripod and full-resolution capture before downsampling.
- [ ] Set phone brightness manually, hide private notifications, use a neutral display name, and keep SSIDs/passwords, MACs and IP addresses out of footage. Do not record password entry.
- [ ] Capture real two-shots plus clean screen recordings. Sync with a visible tap/channel change and retain the observed delay. Record horizontal and vertical takes, not just crops.
- [ ] Hold final results for several seconds. Capture one uninterrupted Send-completion/phone-shutdown/panel-playback proof take. Back up originals before editing.
- [ ] Use licensed audio/cover art, original Glyph sprites, provider credits where shown, and an Android/iOS coverage note in the upload description.

### Android screen recording over USB

Install Android SDK Platform Tools, enable USB debugging and accept this computer's prompt. Use USB during setup filming because Wi-Fi handoff can interrupt wireless adb. Check [Android's adb recording documentation](https://developer.android.com/tools/adb#screenrecord) for device-specific options; native `screenrecord` captures video without audio and does not support changing rotation during recording.

```bash
adb devices
adb shell screenrecord --time-limit 60 --bit-rate 12000000 /sdcard/glyph-hero.mp4
adb pull /sdcard/glyph-hero.mp4 ./glyph-hero-screen.mp4

adb shell screenrecord --time-limit 30 --bit-rate 12000000 /sdcard/glyph-setup.mp4
adb pull /sdcard/glyph-setup.mp4 ./glyph-setup-screen.mp4
```

Keep the phone portrait for the app recording; use it as an inset in the landscape edit. Stop early with Ctrl+C and wait for the recorder to finish before pulling. Repeat with distinct filenames for Display and Now Playing. Record any needed audio separately. Play back each pulled file to check for dropped frames, legible text and private information before deleting the device copy.
