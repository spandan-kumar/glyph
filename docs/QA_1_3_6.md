## Cleanup and limits

Stopped Music after the screen-off check and confirmed no running microphone,
streaming foreground service or Glyph power lock before restarting the app.
Removed test Saved ID 2 and its GIF after switching away from the Image effect.
Original playback fields, complete configuration and Saved JSON matched the
snapshots; the original file inventory and non-preset file sizes also matched.
The preset JSON file can retain padding after deletion; its parsed content
matched exactly. Both temporary imported creations and their external fixture
files were deleted. Automatic catalog checks were restored to disabled;
restarting reset the session-only Music toggles. Glyph reopened on Halloween
Grid with the device off, matching the initial state.
Both Music toggles were then read back as disabled; after leaving Music,
microphone, streaming service and Glyph power locks were checked again and
were absent. The final app log contained no fatal, unhandled or out-of-memory
exception.

Final battery temperature was 28°C. This is a single measurement, not proof of
steady-state temperature under sustained load.

Evidence is local, outside the repository, under
`/tmp/glyph-full-qa-2026-10-10` and
`/tmp/glyph-phone-qa-2026-10-10`; these contain read-back GIFs, screenshots,
frame records and private controller snapshots. Do not publish the snapshots.

- A 64×64 controller and additional storage hardware were not available.
  Its preparation budget is covered by tests and computer-side full bakes;
  this does not establish available filesystem space on that hardware.
- Missing/failing native power channels were injected in automated tests,
  not into the installed release app. Normal resource cleanup was checked
  on the phone.
- Daily-check timing is covered by automated tests; this QA did not wait
  24 hours. Actual signed delivery, persistence and offline recovery were
  checked on the phone.
- The shared GIF's frame preservation/upscale behavior was checked by
  encoder tests; only the Android share sheet was exercised on the phone.
- iOS and the proposed setup wizard were not built or physically tested.
  The wizard remains a plan with a spare-controller contract-test gate.
- This is functional/lifecycle QA, not a multi-hour thermal soak.

No commit, push or release tag was made by this QA pass.
