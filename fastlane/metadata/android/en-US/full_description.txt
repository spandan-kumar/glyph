Glyph sets up your WLED LED matrix and brings it to life. A guided wizard gets a new display working, then you swipe through more than 13,000 animations like TV channels, draw your own pixel art, show the time, put your music on the wall and play games, all from your phone. Free, open source, no account.

Your matrix lives at the top of the screen as a glowing live panel. Swipe it to flip through animations, roll the dice for a surprise, and tap Send to put an animation on the device so it keeps playing with your phone off.

SET UP
• Join a new device to your home Wi-Fi, pick its size, and find its wiring with three taps of a light test. Glyph writes the layout to the device for you.
• Grids of panels: panel order, upside-down panels, power and frame-rate tips.
• Saved animations, WLED effects and live streams all come out the right way up. A final check, and undo anytime.
• Devices set their own clock from your phone's time zone, so Routines run on time.

DISPLAY
• 13,597 animations in subject groups such as Animals, Nature, Holidays, Gaming, Flags and Patterns: 62 procedural effects (plasma, fire, aurora, reaction–diffusion, falling sand…), 356 original and CC0 pixel-art sprites, Famous Classics (public-domain characters and paintings such as the 1928 Steamboat Willie mouse, Pinocchio, Alice, Dracula and The Starry Night) and 12,361 animations from an imported pixel-art collection, shared with permission.
• Search the whole library by name, subject, colour or size. Everything is on your phone, so it works offline.
• Tweak colours and motion with a palette reel and rotary knobs.
• Seamless loops on the device and accurate colours: device gamma, true blacks and stable hues at low brightness.

MAKE
• Draw: frames, onion skin, mirror drawing, fill, shapes and undo, or start from a picture or GIF. Your strokes appear on the matrix as you draw.
• Write, Clock and Timer with hand-made pixel fonts. Clocks keep the real time on the device with your phone off.
• Bring a GIF: any GIF, WebP or photo, cropped and tuned for LEDs.
• Music: nine visualisers driven by the microphone, with beat detection. Audio never leaves the phone.
• Now Playing: the cover of whatever your phone is playing (Spotify, YouTube Music, any player) with a progress bar, live on the matrix.
• Play: Snake, Blocks, Brick Breaker, Pong, Flap, Racer and Invaders, with your phone as the controller.

DEVICE
• Power, brightness and night light.
• Saved animations, Shows and Routines that run without your phone.
• The Glyph intro plays every time your device powers on.
• Home Assistant blueprints show a Saved look or Show when something happens, then go back afterwards.
• Glance cards for weather, forecast and air quality.
• Storage alerts, multiple devices, a home-screen widget, and the full WLED settings inside the app.

PRIVATE, FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
• No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Glyph talks straight to the devices on your Wi-Fi; your drawings and audio stay on your phone. Weather cards look up your forecast only if you turn them on.
• Glyph's code is free and open source under the MIT license.

WHAT YOU NEED
• A controller with WLED already installed (install.wled.me), driving an LED matrix. Glyph sets up the layout. Complex setups, such as several outputs or custom LED maps, keep their existing configuration. Any panel size works, including HUB75 panels on WLED's HUB75 builds.
• Your phone on the same Wi-Fi.
• Live streaming works on every WLED controller; sending animations to the device needs an ESP32 on WLED 16 or newer.
